What you learn

Seven skill areas, taught in small groups

Every one of them is hands-on. You are on a computer, not reading about one. Before you start, we check what you already know, so your training starts at the right level for you.

Computer basics

The mouse and the keyboard, files and folders, and changing the settings on your machine.

Internet, email, and online communication

Searching, browsing safely, writing professional email, and meeting online.

Microsoft Office and Google Workspace

Documents, spreadsheets, and slides, in both of the tool sets employers use.

Online safety and cybersecurity basics

Passwords, phishing, scams, and protecting your accounts and personal information.

AI literacy

How to use AI tools at work, what they are good at, and where they get things wrong.

Money management

Banking on your phone, keeping a budget, and spotting a scam before it costs you.

Getting ready for work

Your resume, applying online, practicing interviews, and a job search you can keep going on your own.

What you leave with

Proof of your skills, and a plan

A credential

You work toward an industry-recognized credential, including an AI literacy micro-credential.

A finished resume

Your resume, ready to send, and a professional email address to send it from.

Interview practice

You practice with us first, so the first real interview is not your first one.

A job search plan

Where to look, what to apply for, and how to keep the search going.

Someone in your corner after

You keep working with us one to one, we connect you with employers, and after you start a job we check in at 30, 60, and 90 days.

Support while you train

You are not doing this alone

  • In your first week, you and your advisor write a plan together. It says what you are aiming for and what you need to get there.
  • Small groups, so you get time with the instructor.
  • Staff who speak English and Spanish.

Help with the things that get in the way

Depending on what you need, we may be able to help with:

  • Getting online at home.
  • Getting to class.
  • Clothes for an interview.
  • Exam and ID fees.

We work out what you need in your plan, in your first week. There are no stipends or payments for attending.

En español

Clases de computación sin costo, en Lawrenceville. Inscripciones próximamente.

Si usted recibe TANF o SNAP y vive en Nueva Jersey, puede tomar estas clases sin pagar nada. Antes de empezar, revisamos lo que usted ya sabe, para que la clase vaya a su nivel. Después aprende lo básico de la computadora, internet y correo electrónico, Microsoft Office y Google Workspace, cómo protegerse en línea, inteligencia artificial, y cómo manejar su dinero. Al final tiene un certificado que le sirve para buscar trabajo.

El programa dura de 5 a 6 semanas. Es presencial, aquí en Lawrenceville, en grupos pequeños, y también tiene apoyo por internet. Empezamos grupos nuevos con frecuencia. Llámenos y le decimos cuándo empieza el próximo.

¿Quién califica? Usted vive en Nueva Jersey, tiene 18 años o más, recibe TANF o SNAP, y vive en el condado de Hunterdon, Middlesex, Monmouth, Mercer, Ocean, Somerset o Union. Nosotros confirmamos su elegibilidad con la agencia de servicios sociales de su condado. Usted no tiene que hacer ese trámite.

No necesita que nadie lo mande. No hace falta una referencia de un trabajador social. Llame y hablamos directamente con usted.

También le ayudamos a buscar trabajo. Le ayudamos con su currículum, con las solicitudes en internet, y practicamos la entrevista con usted. Después de que empiece a trabajar, lo llamamos a los 30, 60 y 90 días para ver cómo le va.

Según lo que usted necesite, a veces podemos ayudar con el transporte, con ropa para la entrevista, con conexión a internet en casa, y con el costo del examen o de su identificación. Eso lo vemos juntos en su plan, en la primera semana. No hay pagos ni ayuda económica por asistir.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Does it cost anything?
No. It is grant funded, so if you qualify it costs you nothing. To be straight with you: there are no stipends or payments for attending either.
When can I start?
Digital Literacy Training is enrolling soon. Call us at (609) 712-5499 and we will tell you when the next group starts, or ask us to contact you when enrollment opens.
Who is eligible?
You need to live in New Jersey, be 18 or older, currently get TANF or SNAP through WorkFirst New Jersey, and live in Hunterdon, Middlesex, Monmouth, Mercer, Ocean, Somerset, or Union county. We confirm it with your county social services agency.
Do I need a referral?
No. You do not need a caseworker to send you. Call us at (609) 712-5499 and we will start with you directly.
When are the information sessions?
Details on upcoming information sessions will be shared soon. Call us and we will tell you as soon as the next one is set.
Do I need my own computer?
No. You use our computers here. If getting online at home is a problem, tell us, and we will see what we can do as part of your plan.
How long is it, and what are the hours?
About 5 to 6 weeks. Call us and we will tell you the days and hours for the next group.
Is it in person?
Yes. Training is in person at our Lawrenceville campus, with online support. Groups are small.
Will you help me find a job?
Yes. You work with someone one to one on your resume, on where to apply, and on interviews, and we pass on job leads and employer contacts. After you start a job we check in with you at 30, 60, and 90 days.
Can you help with getting to class, or clothes for an interview?
Maybe. Depending on what you need, we may be able to help with getting to class, clothes for an interview, getting online at home, and exam or ID fees. We work that out with you in your plan in your first week. There are no stipends or payments for attending.
Do you have anyone who speaks Spanish?
Yes. We have staff who speak English and Spanish. Call (609) 712-5499 and ask for someone who speaks Spanish.
What if I receive benefits but I am not sure which kind?
Call us at (609) 712-5499 and we will help you work it out. We check with your county social services agency, so you do not have to.
